Loop

Loop: Store operations, in your pocket.

Loop is an AI-powered restaurant operations platform that helps operators monitor performance, identify issues, and take action across their locations. Designed for operators who needed to stay connected to operations away from their desks with instant high-priority alerts, live issue tracking, and key AI interactions available directly on their phones.

PlatformAI-powered restaurant operations
On mobileHigh-priority alerts · Live issue tracking · AI assistant
01 — Why mobile first

Operation alerts had to live on the phone

A

Operators aren't at their desks

Store managers spend the day on the floor and moving between locations. An alert that waits on a laptop is an alert seen too late.

B

Downtime is counted in minutes

When a store goes offline on a delivery platform, orders are lost until someone acts. So each alert shows how long the store has been down and what it's costing.

C

Act from the alert, not after it

Every alert opens into live status and the next step: restore, call the store, snooze, or hand it to Loop's AI assistant. Most issues close in a few taps.

02 — Interactive prototype

Try the mobile app

What to try
  1. Sign in. The fields are already filled in.
  2. Open a store to see its live status and latest updates.
  3. Tap the bell, then open an offline or failed alert to drill into it.
  4. Tap the Loop button at the bottom right to call or chat with the AI assistant.
  5. Open "State of the union" on a store for the weekly ops report.
